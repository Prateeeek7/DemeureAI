# Visitor and Guest Policy
## Section 1: Visiting Hours and Eligible Visitors
1. Parents, registered guardians and approved guests may visit between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM on all days.
2. Visitors meet residents in the ground-floor visitor lounge of each block. Visitors are not permitted inside resident rooms, and no visitor may enter an opposite-gender block.
3. Overnight stays by visitors are not permitted inside the hostel. Parents travelling from outside the city may book the campus guest house through the warden's office.

## Section 2: Visitor Pass Procedure
1. Residents must request a visitor pass in Demeure AI under Student Services at least 12 hours before the visit, giving the visitor's name, the visit date and time, and the purpose.
2. The warden approves or denies each request. On approval the resident receives a gate pass code (format GP-XXXXXX) to share with the visitor.
3. At the SOC Gate the visitor must show the gate pass code and a government-issued photo ID. Visitors without an approved pass are not admitted.
4. Every visitor entry and exit is recorded in the hash-chained gate ledger.

## Section 3: Visitor Conduct
1. Residents are responsible for the conduct of their visitors throughout the visit.
2. Visitors must leave the hostel premises by 7:00 PM. Repeated overstays lead to suspension of the resident's visitor privileges for 30 days.
