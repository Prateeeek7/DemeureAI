# Leave of Absence and Outpass Policy
## Section 1: Applying for Leave
1. Residents apply for leave in Demeure AI under Student Services by entering the start date, end date and destination or reason.
2. Leave requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before departure, except for medical or family emergencies.
3. A single leave request cannot exceed 90 days. Longer absences require a written application to the Chief Warden.

## Section 2: Leaves Exceeding 3 Days
1. Leaves longer than 3 days must include a supporting document (for example a medical certificate, event invitation or travel ticket), uploaded with the request as a PDF, PNG or JPG file of up to 5 MB.
2. The resident's parent or registered guardian is notified by e-mail as soon as the request is submitted, and must confirm consent before the warden approves.
3. Unused mess balances from approved leave are refunded at the end of the year or carried forward, as set out in the Fee Structure.

## Section 3: Approval and Return
1. The warden approves or denies each request. The decision is shown under My Applications and sent to the resident by e-mail.
2. Residents must return by 10:00 PM on the last day of leave and record their entry at the SOC Gate. Late returns are treated as late entries under the Disciplinary Code.
